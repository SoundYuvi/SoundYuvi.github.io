Frequently asked questions

My streak reset unexpectedly. What happened?

Streaks are based on consecutive daily check-ins. If you didn't mark a habit on a given day, the streak resets. Make sure to check in before midnight each day, or enable a reminder so you don't forget.

How do I set a reminder for a habit?

Tap any habit row to open the Edit screen. Toggle on "Enable reminder" and pick your preferred time. Each habit can have its own independent reminder.

Is my data backed up?

Habito stores data locally on your iPhone using UserDefaults, which is included in your standard iOS device backup. If you back up your iPhone via iCloud or Finder, your habits are backed up too.

How do I archive a habit without deleting it?

Swipe left on a habit row — you'll see both "Archive" (yellow) and "Delete" (red) options. Archived habits hide from the main view but keep all their history. Restore them anytime in Settings → Archived Habits.

Can I add a note when I check in?

Yes! After tapping the checkmark, a note prompt appears automatically. You can also swipe right on any habit row and tap "Note" to add one at any time.

How do I change the app's theme colour?

Go to the Settings tab (gear icon) and pick from 7 accent colours: Indigo, Blue, Purple, Pink, Orange, Teal and Green.

Does Habito sync across my devices?

Currently Habito stores data locally on each device. iCloud sync is on the roadmap and will be added in a future update.
