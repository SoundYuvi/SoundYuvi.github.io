நாற்பது – Tamil Wisdom

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 June 2026

நாற்பது – Tamil Wisdom is an app for reading and reflecting on the Pathinen Keelkanakku Noolgal — classical Tamil ethical poetry. The app is free, contains no ads or tracking, and does not collect or share any personal data.

1. What data the app handles

The app stores only what you create on your device — your favourite verses, your preferences (theme, notification settings), and your daily reminder time. All verse text is bundled with the app; no content is fetched from external servers.

2. Where your data is stored

All data is stored locally on your device. We do not operate any server that receives, stores, or processes your data.

3. No accounts, no tracking, no ads

The app has no login and no user accounts. We do not collect personal information, do not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and do not track you across apps or websites. No data is sold or shared with any third party.

4. Notifications

If you enable daily reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. These are generated entirely on-device and are not sent through any external server.

5. Network

The app works fully offline. No internet connection is required to read verses, save favourites, or use any core feature.

6. Your control

You can remove favourites at any time in the app, or delete the app to remove all stored data from your device.

7. Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date.
