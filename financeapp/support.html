Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?
On your device and your personal iCloud (Apple's private, encrypted database). It never reaches our servers — we don't have any.
Does my data sync between my iPhone and iPad?
Yes — as long as both are signed into the same iCloud account with iCloud Drive enabled, your data syncs automatically and privately.
What happens if I delete the app?
Local data is removed. Data already synced to iCloud stays in your iCloud until you delete it from Settings → iCloud, and returns if you reinstall.
What does Pro include?
Unlimited accounts, investments & net worth, the subscriptions tracker, home-screen widgets, full analytics and multi-currency. Every plan (monthly, yearly, lifetime) unlocks the same features.
How does the 7-day free trial work?
The yearly plan starts with 7 days free. Cancel anytime before it ends in your Apple subscription settings and you won't be charged.
How do I restore my purchase on a new device?
Open the app → Profile → Restore Purchases, signed into the same Apple ID you bought with.
How do I cancel a subscription?
iPhone Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → Northstar → Cancel. (Lifetime is a one-time purchase and never renews.)
