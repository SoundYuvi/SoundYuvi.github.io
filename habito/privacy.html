🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

The short version

Habito does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data to any server. Everything stays on your device.

What data does Habito store?

Habito stores your habits, streaks, completion history, notes and settings locally on your iPhone using the device's own storage (UserDefaults). This data never leaves your device and is never sent to us or any third party.

Do you collect analytics or crash reports?

No. Habito contains no analytics SDKs, no crash reporters, no advertising frameworks and no third-party code of any kind. We have zero visibility into how you use the app.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, Habito schedules local notifications on your device using iOS's built-in notification system. No data about your notification preferences leaves your device.

No accounts required

Habito has no sign-up, no login, and no user accounts. You do not need to provide an email address or any personal information to use the app.

Children's privacy

Habito does not collect any data from anyone, including children. It complies with COPPA and similar regulations by design.

Changes to this policy

If we ever update this policy, the new version will be posted here. Because we collect no data, meaningful changes are unlikely.
