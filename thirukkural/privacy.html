Thirukkural

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 June 2026

Thirukkural – Tamil Wisdom is an app for reading, listening to and reflecting on the 1330 couplets of the Thirukkural. Your privacy is fully protected: the app is free, contains no ads or tracking, and does not sell your data.

1. What data the app handles

The app stores only what you create — your favourite couplets, reflections, quiz progress, badges, streaks and preferences (language mode, theme, card styles). The Thirukkural text and audio are bundled with the app.

2. Where your data is stored

On iOS, your data is stored locally on your device and may sync privately across your Apple devices via iCloud, governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

On Android, your data is stored locally on your device. Firebase (by Google) is used for app analytics and crash reporting to help us improve stability and performance. Firebase may collect device information and usage data as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Gemini AI (Android)

The Android app uses Google's Gemini AI to power AI-assisted features such as kural explanations and reflections. When you use these features, your input (the kural text or your query) is sent to Google's Gemini API to generate a response. No personally identifiable information is intentionally sent. Google's use of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Gemini API Terms of Service.

4. No accounts, no ads

The app has no login and no user accounts. We do not use advertising SDKs and do not sell your data to any third party.

5. Leaderboard

Leaderboard rankings are handled through Apple Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games (Android) using your game nickname — never your real identity. Participation is optional.

6. Donations

Donations are optional and processed entirely by Apple (iOS) or Google (Android) through their respective stores. We never see your payment details.

7. Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. They are generated on-device and not sent through any external server.

8. Network

The core app works fully offline. An internet connection is only needed for AI features, leaderboards, or processing a donation.

9. Your control

You can remove favourites and reflections at any time, or delete the app to remove all local data.

10. Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date.

12. Contact
