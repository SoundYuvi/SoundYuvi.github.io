Frequently asked questions

Is the app free?
Tasko is free to use, with an optional Pro upgrade for advanced features like unlimited projects, templates and assignments.
Does it work offline?
Yes. You can add, edit and complete tasks without an internet connection — changes sync once you're back online.
How does AI Quick Capture work?
Just type or speak a task naturally, like "Submit report Friday 5pm" — Tasko automatically detects the date, time and priority.
Can I see my tasks in Kanban or Calendar view?
Yes — switch between a drag-and-drop Kanban board, a calendar/timeline view, or a classic list, whichever fits your workflow.
Is there an Apple Watch app?
Yes — view and complete tasks right from your wrist, with watch complications support.
Can I share or assign tasks to someone else?
Yes — assign tasks to collaborators and keep track of progress with comments and notifications.
Where is my data saved?
On your device, and synced privately via your own iCloud if enabled. It's never sent to our servers.
