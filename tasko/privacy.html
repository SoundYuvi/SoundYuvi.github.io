Tasko

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 June 2026

Tasko is a task manager for iPhone and iPad that helps you capture, organise and complete tasks. Your privacy is built into the app's design: your tasks stay in your own private iCloud and are never sent to our servers or any third party.

1. What data the app handles

The app stores the records you create — tasks, projects, templates, comments, attachments and reminders — together with your app preferences (theme, notification settings).

2. Where your data is stored

  • On your device using Apple's on-device storage.
  • In your private iCloud (Apple CloudKit) so your data syncs across your own Apple devices, including the Apple Watch app. This data is stored in your iCloud account and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We have no access to it.

We do not operate any server that receives, stores, or processes your task data.

3. No accounts, no tracking

The app has no login and no user accounts. We do not collect personal information, do not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and do not track you across apps or websites.

4. Sharing & assigning tasks

If you choose to share or assign a task to someone else, only the task details you choose to share are sent to that person via Apple's frameworks (e.g. iCloud sharing, notifications). We do not see or store this information on any server we control.

5. Purchases

Pro subscriptions and the lifetime unlock are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Purchase validation happens on-device via Apple's StoreKit; we do not run a purchase server.

6. Notifications

With your permission, the app schedules local notifications (task reminders, Focus session alerts) on your device. These are generated on-device and are not sent through any external server.

7. Your control

You can edit or delete any task at any time. To remove all synced data, sign out of iCloud for the app in Settings → Apple ID → iCloud, or delete the app. Deleting data on one device removes it from your iCloud and your other devices.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date.
