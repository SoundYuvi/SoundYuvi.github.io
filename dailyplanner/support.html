Frequently asked questions

Is the app free?
Yes — DailyPlanner is completely free with no ads and no tracking.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Everything is stored locally on your device — no internet connection is ever needed.
How do I add a task?
Tap the text field at the bottom of the tasks page, type your task and hit return. Tap a task to check it off, or swipe to delete.
How do I add an event to the timeline?
Tap the + button on the timeline page, then enter the event name, start time and duration. You can also enable a reminder notification.
How do I navigate to a different day?
Tap the calendar icon at the top of the tasks page to open the date picker, then select any date.
Can I adjust the text size?
Yes — tap the settings icon and choose your preferred text size. The change applies across all three pages.
Is there an iPad version?
Yes — on iPad the app shows a full two-page spread with tasks on the left and the timeline and notes side by side on the right, just like a real planner.
Where is my data stored?
All your tasks, events and notes are stored locally on your device only. Nothing is sent to our servers or any third party.
