FinanceApp

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 June 2026

FinanceApp is a personal finance app for iPhone and iPad that helps you track expenses, income, accounts, budgets, investments, and net worth. Your privacy is built into the app's design: your financial data stays in your own private iCloud and is never sent to our servers or any third party.

1. What data the app handles

The app stores the records you create — transactions, accounts, budgets, categories, investments, savings/retirement plans, net-worth items, subscriptions, and split-expense groups — together with your app preferences (currency, theme, display name).

2. Where your data is stored

  • On your device using Apple's SwiftData.
  • In your private iCloud (Apple CloudKit) so your data syncs across your own Apple devices. This data is stored in your iCloud account and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We have no access to it.

We do not operate any server that receives, stores, or processes your financial data.

3. No accounts, no tracking

The app has no login and no user accounts. We do not collect personal information, do not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and do not track you across apps or websites.

4. Network requests (market data only)

To show live prices, the app fetches public market data from third-party services: currency exchange rates, stock prices, and mutual fund NAVs. These requests contain only the symbols being looked up (e.g. a stock ticker). No personal or financial data about you is ever sent to these services.

5. Purchases

Pro subscriptions and the lifetime unlock are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Purchase validation happens on-device via Apple's StoreKit; we do not run a purchase server.

6. Notifications

With your permission, the app schedules local notifications (budget alerts, bill and subscription reminders) on your device. These are generated on-device and are not sent through any external server.

7. Your control

You can edit or delete any item in the app at any time. To remove all synced data, sign out of iCloud for the app in Settings → Apple ID → iCloud, or delete the app. Deleting data on one device removes it from your iCloud and your other devices.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date.
