Frequently asked questions

Is the app free?
Yes — நாற்பது is completely free with no ads and no tracking.
What is Pathinen Keelkanakku Noolgal?
It is the collection of 18 classical Tamil ethical texts, each containing 40 verses (நாற்பது). This app brings them to life starting with Iniyavai Naarpadhu, with more texts coming soon.
Which texts are available right now?
Iniyavai Naarpadhu (இனியவை நாற்பது) is fully available. Inna Naarpadhu, Kaar Naarpadhu and Kalavazhi Naarpadhu are coming soon.
Does it work offline?
Yes — all verse text is bundled with the app. No internet connection is needed to read or explore.
What is the Verse of the Day?
Each day a fresh verse is shown from across all available texts to inspire your reading.
How does the Card Maker work?
Tap any verse and use the Card Maker to turn it into a beautiful image you can share with friends or on social media.
Where are my favourites saved?
On your device only. They are never sent to our servers.
