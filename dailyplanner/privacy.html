Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

DailyPlanner stores all your tasks, events and notes locally on your device. We do not collect, store, or share any personal data on our servers.

Data Storage

Everything you add to DailyPlanner — tasks, scheduled events, and notes — is stored only on your device using Apple's local storage. Nothing is sent to external servers.

No Accounts Required

DailyPlanner has no login and no user accounts. You can use the full app from day one without providing any personal information.

Notifications

With your permission, the app schedules local notifications for your events. These are generated entirely on your device and are not sent through any external server.

No Analytics or Tracking

No analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking of any kind is used in this app.

No Third-Party Sharing

No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date.
