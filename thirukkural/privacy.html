Thirukkural

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 June 2026

Thirukkural – Tamil Wisdom is an app for reading, listening to and reflecting on the 1330 couplets of the Thirukkural. Your privacy is fully protected: the app is free, collects no personal data, works offline, and contains no ads or tracking.

1. What data the app handles

The app stores only what you create on your own device — your favourite couplets, your written reflections, your quiz progress, badges and streaks, and your preferences (language mode, theme, card styles). The Thirukkural text and audio are bundled with the app.

2. Where your data is stored

Your favourites, reflections, progress and preferences are stored locally on your device. If iCloud is enabled, some of this may sync privately across your own Apple devices through your iCloud account, governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We have no access to this data and operate no server.

3. No accounts, no tracking

The app has no login and no user accounts. We do not collect personal information, do not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and do not track you across apps or websites.

4. Leaderboard

If the app offers a leaderboard, any ranking is handled through Apple's Game Center using your Game Center nickname — never your real identity. Participation is optional and governed by Apple's terms. You can disable Game Center in iPhone Settings at any time.

5. Donations

Donations are optional and processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details, and donating is never required to use any feature.

6. Notifications

If you enable reminders (such as "Kural of the Day"), the app schedules local notifications on your device. They are generated on-device and not sent through any external server.

7. Network

The core app works fully offline. An internet connection is only needed for optional features such as Game Center leaderboards or processing a donation.

8. Your control

You can remove favourites and reflections at any time, or delete the app to remove all local data. If synced via iCloud, signing out of iCloud for the app in Settings → Apple ID → iCloud removes the synced copy.

9. Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date.
