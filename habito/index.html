🔒 Privacy isn't a feature. It's the foundation.

Habito has no login and no backend. Your habit data lives only on your iPhone — we never see it, never sync it to our servers, and there are no ads or analytics SDKs. Your streaks are yours alone.

How it works

Up and running in under a minute.

1

Add your habits

Give each habit a name, an emoji icon, a category and an optional daily reminder time.

2

Check in daily

Tap the circle to mark a habit done. Your streak grows — and confetti flies when you go perfect.

3

Track your progress

Flip to the Stats tab to see your 90-day heatmap, completion rates and longest streaks.

4

Stay consistent

Weekly overview, category filters and per-habit notes help you understand your patterns and improve.
